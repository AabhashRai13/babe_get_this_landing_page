Questions, answered

Is Babe, Get This free?

Yes. The app is free on Google Play, with no ads and no data sales.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet, but the iOS app is on the way. Leave your email in the form above and we'll send you one email the day it's on the App Store.

How do we share a shopping list?

Open a list and share its code with your partner. Once they join, you both see the same list and every change syncs live.

How does voice capture work?

Tap record and say what you need, as messily as you like. The app turns it into tidy list items with quantities and categories. Voice capture needs an internet connection and an account.

Who can see my lists?

Lists you don't share stay on your phone and are never uploaded. Only lists you share are stored on our server, so the people you share them with can see and edit them.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You sign up with an email address. Voice transcription runs on our servers, and the account is there to prevent abuse of that, not to profile you.
